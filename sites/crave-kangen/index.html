The Water

Not a brand of water — a machine that makes it.

Enagic's system runs your tap water through medical-grade filtration, then splits it with electrolysis across platinum-coated titanium plates. The result is a range of waters at different pH levels — including hydrogen-rich Kangen Water® you drink within moments of pouring.

  • Hydrogen-rich, at the sourceMolecular hydrogen (H₂) dissolves as the water ionizes — freshest the instant it's poured, which is why we pour it in front of you.
  • Filtered first, alwaysSediment, chlorine, and taste-and-odor compounds are reduced before any water reaches your glass.
  • Five waters, one countertopDrinking, beauty, cooking, and cleaning waters from a single system that installs at your sink.

Filtration
